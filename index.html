Advanced Infrastructure & Sustainable Processing

Fully Automated Processing

End-to-end automated cardamom processing system powered entirely by electricity. No traditional wood-burning methods are used, ensuring controlled drying, consistent moisture levels, and superior color preservation.

AI-Enabled 4-Way Sorting

Equipped with advanced 4-way grading and AI-assisted sorting technology to deliver precise size calibration, uniform color selection, and export-grade batch consistency.

Power Security & Backup

Supported by a dedicated 200 kV transformer and 200 kV industrial backup generator to ensure uninterrupted operations and timely order fulfillment.
